Strange Encounters Sayn

An experimental psychological, bizarro and absurdist multimedia project.

The project

Strange Encounters Sayn is the project that Sayn belongs to. It is a massive one, and it is multimedia: the plan is to tell it through novels, comics, games, and perhaps one day an anime series of its own.
