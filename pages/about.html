What is Sayn?

Sayn is a character, a symbol and a denpa-inspired style of fiction. It is also a project far bigger than this site: one story, planned across many media.

Sayn

Sayn is the character that Strange Encounters Sayn is built around. The name also stands for the style of the project and for the kind of fiction it is: psychological, absurd, and inspired by denpa.

The project

Sayn is a massive project. The plan is to tell it in more than one medium: novels, comics, games, and perhaps one day an anime series of its own.

Rule 116

Sayn is an internet phenomenon: the kind of thing that turns up wherever you look. Its number is 116, from the rule that goes with it.

Rule 116 of the internet: Sayn is always there.

The number works as a symbol of its own. Where you see 116, it means Sayn.

Denpa

Denpa (電波, “radio waves”) is a Japanese term for fiction about people who seem to be tuned to signals no one else receives: stories of dissociation, paranoia and realities that stop agreeing with each other.

The symbol

The Sayn symbol is the mark at the top of every page. It comes in a green version and a black-and-white version, and the gallery has both as icons and as desktop and mobile wallpapers.
